Hover over or Tab to a library link to preview it; Escape closes the preview. Each article ends with the documents that link to it. Select a passage to highlight, comment, or leave a warning. Click a passage’s margin mark or a pip on the progress rail to open its thread. The footer switches between continuous and two-page reading. On narrow screens, pages appear one at a time. Your place is saved automatically for this participant and edition. PDF text can be selected and searched; passage annotations currently support Markdown.
