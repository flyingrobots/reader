Lantern 0.9 in the field

Six weeks of sync on real devices: how often highlights arrived, how long catch-up took, and where anchors went missing.

Summary

Between the 0.9 release and the end of the trial, fourteen volunteer readers used Lantern on two to four devices each. They made 9,412 highlights and 2,086 margin notes. Every event that was written on one device arrived on every other device that came online; none was lost.

Two findings changed the plan. First, catch-up time is dominated by re-anchoring against revised sources, not by the network. Second, readers read an orphaned note as lost work even when its quote was preserved, so the interface has to show what the note was written against.

Recommendations for 1.0

  1. Keep relay events for thirty days, configurable, with a seven-day floor.
  2. Show the edition an orphaned note was written against, one click away.
  3. Cap retry attempts; a device in a drawer should go quiet, not drain its battery.
  4. Measure re-anchoring separately from transfer in every catch-up report.
