# What Note G costs a reader

**Opinion:** read the first two paragraphs of [Note G](../analytical-engine/lovelace-note-g.md) and the last one, then stop. The middle is a table you will not run in your head.

**Coverage:** I read the whole note once and the opening three times.

**Checked:** the closing value. Her $B_7$ is $-\tfrac{1}{30}$; I ran the recurrence in [my notes](../analytical-engine/reading-note-g.md) and got the same.

The page asks for about twenty minutes. Five of them are worth it for almost anyone; the other fifteen are for people who want to see a program written before there were programs.
