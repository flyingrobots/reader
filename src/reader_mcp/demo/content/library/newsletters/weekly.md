# The Weekly

*Margins, engines and the ordinary mind.*

## This week's pick

Virginia Woolf's [Modern Fiction](../essayists/woolf/modern-fiction.md) asks us to "examine for a moment an ordinary mind on an ordinary day," and then shows how much the novel of her time left out. It is the essay of the week because three other pieces this week argue, in their own ways, about what a reader keeps.

## Also worth your evening

- The Archivist on [keeping the margins](../reading-room/on-keeping-the-margins.md) when the text beneath them changes.
- The Lantern team's revised sync design, now with a relay that keeps events for thirty days.
- Ada Lovelace's [Note G](../analytical-engine/lovelace-note-g.md), with the Plain Reader's [notes on reading it as a program](../analytical-engine/reading-note-g.md).
- Thoreau's [Where I Lived, and What I Lived For](../walden/thoreau/where-i-lived.md), for anyone who needs to hear "Simplify, simplify" this week.
